Everything to tell your story

The tools of a desktop editor, in one simple app.

Edit

  • Split, trim, duplicate and reorder your clips
  • Transitions on one cut or all of them
  • Reverse, freeze a frame, crop, straighten

Pace

  • Speed from 0.1x to 100x
  • Curves to speed up and slow down in one shot
  • Cut clips to the beat of the music

Write and animate

  • Titles with fonts, outlines, shadows and animations
  • Automatic captions, editable as a list
  • Keyframes to animate any element

Layer and polish

  • Picture in picture, stickers and your images
  • Green screen, masks and blend modes
  • Filters, brightness, contrast, warmth, vignette

Add sound

  • Music from your library or from Files
  • Voiceover and extract a clip's sound
  • Volume and fades for each element

Export

  • 9:16, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 and more
  • 720p to 4K, 24 to 60 frames per second
  • Save to Photos or share directly
