Privacy Policy

Gold Video Pro — last updated November 10, 2026

Gold Video Pro is a video editing app for iPhone. It is built so your videos stay yours: all editing happens on your phone.

In short

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • Your videos, photos, sounds and projects stay on your iPhone. We don't send them to any server.
  • The app is free and funded by an ad banner shown while a video exports, and nowhere else. It is provided by Google AdMob.
  • Where the law requires it (European Union, European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland), your consent is asked before any ad, and you can change it at any time.
  • No analytics of our own, and no ad tracking across apps (the app doesn't ask for Apple's tracking permission).

Your videos, photos and sounds

The media you pick are copied into the app's private storage on your iPhone, with your projects. They only leave it when you decide to: by saving an exported video to your photo library, or by sharing it with the app of your choice.

Deleting a project or the app erases these copies from the device.

Permissions

  • Photos: pick videos and photos (only the ones you select), and save your finished videos.
  • Microphone: record a voiceover, only when you start recording.
  • Speech recognition: make automatic captions from the speech in your video, when you ask for it. Recognition happens on the iPhone when the language allows it. When it can't for the chosen language, iOS sends the sound to Apple to transcribe it, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Music library: add songs you bought or imported in the Music app to your videos, when you pick one. The chosen song is copied into the app; nothing is sent anywhere.

You can withdraw these permissions at any time in Settings > Gold Video.

Imported fonts and synthetic voices

Fonts you import are kept in the app, on your iPhone. Reading a title aloud uses the voices installed on the device.

Advertising and consent

While a video exports, the app shows an ad banner provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). No ad appears while you edit.

Consent. At first launch, if you are in a region where the law requires it (European Union, European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland), a Google consent form is shown before any ad. You can accept, refuse or choose in detail. You can change your mind at any time in the app: Settings > Privacy > Ad choices. Without your consent, Google may show non-personalised ads, or no ads.

Data processed by Google. To show, cap and measure ads and to fight fraud, Google's kit may process technical information: device model and system, IP address (which gives an approximate location), language, the app's identifier, and your interactions with ads (views, taps). The app doesn't ask for Apple's tracking permission: the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used. Google processes this data under its own policy: how Google uses information from partner apps.

We only receive overall, anonymous statistics from Google (number of ads shown, revenue). We don't sell any data.

Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them.

Your rights

We hold no data about you: your projects stay on your device, under your control. For advertising data, you can withdraw your consent in the app (Settings > Privacy > Ad choices) and exercise your rights with Google. You can also write to us at the address below, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL, cnil.fr).
